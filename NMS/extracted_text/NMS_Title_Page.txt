Ethical considerations
This study analyzed publicly accessible textual comments and associated metadata from short-video platforms. No direct interaction with human participants occurred, and no personally identifiable information is reproduced in the manuscript. Under the institutional practice governing analysis of fully public, non-interventional textual data, formal ethics review was not required.

Not applicable because the study did not recruit participants or intervene in user behavior.
