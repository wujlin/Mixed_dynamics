Platformed Recognition: Issue Visibility, Affective Evaluation, and Boundary-Making across Douyin and Kuaishou
Table 1. Research hypotheses.
Code
Hypothesis
H1
Douyin and Kuaishou exhibit significantly different issue structures and discursive visibility regimes.
H2
Issue structure significantly predicts affective evaluation on both platforms.
H3
Affective evaluation significantly predicts identity-boundary orientation on both platforms.
H4
Affective evaluation mediates the relationship between issue structure and identity-boundary orientation.
H5
Cross-platform differences are stronger in upstream issue ecology than in the downstream affective mediation mechanism.
