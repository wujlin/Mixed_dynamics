\section{Additional critical-slowing-down diagnostics}
\label{supp:s4-csd}

This note provides additional diagnostics for critical slowing down (CSD) beyond the main-text panels. We report early-warning statistics based on short-lag autocorrelation and variance, and we evaluate multi-lag autocorrelation in agent-based simulations to verify that the rising autocorrelation near the threshold is not an artifact of a single lag choice.

  \label{fig:supp:ews-ac-var}
\end{figure}

\end{figure}
